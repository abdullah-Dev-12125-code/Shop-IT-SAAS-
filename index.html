Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What is your return policy? ▼
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all products. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, simply return it within 30 days for a full refund. Returns are free for items that arrive damaged or defective.
How long does shipping take? ▼
Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days, while expedited shipping takes 2-3 business days. Free shipping is available on orders over $50. International shipping options are also available with varying delivery times.
Is my payment information secure? ▼
Yes, we use industry-leading SSL encryption and PCI DSS compliance to protect your payment information. Your credit card details are never stored on our servers, and we work with trusted payment processors only.
Do you offer bulk discounts? ▼
Yes, we offer special discounts for bulk orders. Please contact our sales team at bulk@shopit.com for a custom quote based on your specific needs and order quantity.
Can I track my order? ▼
Absolutely! You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. You can track your package in real-time through our website or the carrier's website.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers for wholesale orders. All payment methods are secure and encrypted.
